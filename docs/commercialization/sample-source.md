# Q3 Business Review

Q3 revenue grew 15% year over year to 230 million RMB.

Main growth drivers:

- Overseas market revenue increased 28%.
- Enterprise services increased 22%.
- Renewal rate improved to 91%.

Main risks:

- Supply chain shortage delayed delivery by two to three weeks.
- Talent retention pressure increased in AI engineering roles.

Next quarter priorities:

- Expand Southeast Asia pipeline.
- Diversify supply chain partners.
- Launch the AI product line.
