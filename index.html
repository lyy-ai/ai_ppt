End-to-end workflow

From rough material to presentation-ready deck

Best PPT follows the same chain a human consultant would use: understand the brief, shape the outline, generate slides, then preview and download.

1

Upload or describe

Start from a prompt, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, URLs, or mixed source materials.

2

Confirm the plan

AI proposes audience, scene, length, structure, image advice, and speaker-note direction before generation.

3

Preview and export

Review the generated deck online, restore history, and download editable PPTX or narrated PPTX.
